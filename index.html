Method and evidence

Published baseline likelihood: Winther et al. 2020, RF-CL model; symptomatic adults with suspected chronic coronary disease and no documented prior CAD. This is an implementation of the published mathematical equation, not a refitted model. Unknown risk factors and symptom classification produce a scenario envelope, not a confidence interval. The research interface uses conservative, incomplete safety prompts informed by the 2021 chest pain guideline; it is not a guideline implementation or clinical pathway validation.
